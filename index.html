About Us

Nairobi's cleaning partner for homes and businesses.

Witty Cleaning Solutions started with a simple goal: make it easy for busy Nairobi households and businesses to keep their spaces spotless without the hassle. What began as a small residential cleaning service has grown into a full-service team handling everything from routine house cleaning to post-construction clean-ups and fumigation across the city.

Our Mission

To deliver dependable, thorough cleaning services that give Nairobi's homes and businesses more time back in their day.

Our Vision

To be Nairobi's most trusted name in professional cleaning, known for consistency, care, and attention to detail.
